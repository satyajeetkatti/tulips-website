Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains what Tulips Pre Primary School, Ichalkaranji ("the school") collects through this website, the enquiry form and the online admission form, why, and who can see it. The website and the admission form are run with Preschool Desk, the school's office app.

This website

  • You can read the website without signing in. The school does not set cookies on this website.
  • Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify you. Only the school sees the counts.
  • The maps on the Contact page come from Google Maps. A map loads only when you tap "Show map"; then Google's privacy policy applies to it.
  • The Instagram posts in the gallery are copies of the school's own posts. Tapping one opens Instagram.

Enquiry and school visit form

  • What you give: the parent's name and mobile number, the child's name and date of birth, the branch, the school year, any comments, and the visit time you choose.
  • Why: only to contact you about admission by call, WhatsApp or SMS, and to plan your visit.
  • Who sees it: the school staff who handle admissions.

Online admission form

  • What you give: the child's details (name, date of birth, birth place, gender, religion, category, caste, mother tongue, blood group, special needs, allergies, Aadhaar number if given), the parents' or guardian's details (names, mobiles, email, Aadhaar numbers, qualification, occupation), the address, emergency contact and pick-up persons, previous school and siblings, photo consent, and the documents: the child's photo, birth certificate and Aadhaar card, and the parents' Aadhaar cards.
  • Why: to process your child's admission and, once your child joins, as the child's school record.
  • Your form opens only on the phone that started it. On another phone it opens only after a code sent by SMS to a parent's mobile on the form.
  • Uploaded documents are kept in private storage and opened only through links that expire. Aadhaar cards are shown only to the school management (principal and branch heads), not to teachers.
  • The form is filled by the child's parent or guardian, who gives these details and this consent on the child's behalf.
  • Religion, category and caste are asked because school and government records need them. The child's Aadhaar card is optional.
  • Children's photos are shared on the school's social media only if you say yes to photo consent in the form. You can take back photo consent at any time by telling the school; new photos of your child will then not be posted.

Payments and messages

  • The registration fee is paid through Easebuzz, the school's payment gateway. The school never sees your card or UPI details.
  • SMS codes and messages are sent through SMS Country.

Where it is kept and how it is protected

  • On Supabase servers in Mumbai, India. Data is encrypted while it travels (HTTPS) and where it is stored.
  • Each staff member sees only what their work needs. Weekly backups are encrypted.
  • The school does not sell your details or use them for advertising. They are shared only with the services named on this page, so that they can do their part, or where the law requires.
  • If your details are ever exposed in a data breach, the school will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India without delay, with what happened and what you can do.

How long it is kept

  • Enquiries and visit bookings: the school deletes them within one year of the enquiry.
  • Admission forms of children who do not join: the school deletes them, with their documents, within six months of the start of the school year they applied for.
  • Children who join: kept as the school record while the child studies here, and afterwards only as long as the law requires school records to be kept.

Your rights

  • Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask the school for a summary of the details it holds about you and your child, and who they were shared with.
  • You can ask for anything wrong to be corrected, anything missing to be completed, and details to be deleted when they are no longer needed (unless the law requires the school to keep them).
  • You can take back your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. The school will then stop using those details, but what was done before stays valid.
  • You can name another person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself.
  • You can change your admission form yourself until the school confirms the admission. After that, ask the school.
  • To use any of these rights, or to complain, contact the privacy contact below. The school will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with the reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
