English medium

Playgroup · 2+ years

A gentle first step away from home: rhymes, sensory play, colours and making friends.

English medium

Nursery · 3+ years

Talking, listening and fine-motor skills; first letter sounds and counting with objects.

English medium

Jr. KG · 4+ years

Phonics, writing letters, numbers and simple projects about the world around us.

English medium

Sr. KG · 5+ years

Reading simple words, writing, early maths and getting ready for Class 1.
