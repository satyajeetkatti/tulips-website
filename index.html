Our story

Eighty years of Montessori, from Kolhapur to Ichalkaranji

Our founder, Shrimant Maisaheb Bavdekar, trained under Dr Maria Montessori and opened the first pre-primary school in South Maharashtra, in Kolhapur, in 1946. Today Tulips — which carries her name — continues that tradition in three branches in Ichalkaranji — learning by doing, with love and patience.

Why Tulips

Six reasons parents choose us

The Montessori way

Children learn by doing — with their hands and all their senses, at their own pace.

English with phonics

Letter sounds first, so reading and speaking English comes naturally.

Play-way and project method

Rhymes, games, floor mats and projects turn every lesson into play.

Big playground and sandpit

3,000 sq ft of safe outdoor play for strong, happy bodies.

Attention for every child

Parent participation programmes, celebrations and free workshops for parents.
