A day at Tulips

Three happy hours: play, learning and friends.

Every day

A typical day

School hours differ by branch and medium, but the day follows the same pattern everywhere.
English medium: Gaonbhag 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM · Sangli Road 9:30 AM – 12:30 PM.
Marathi medium: Montessori Balmandir, Gaonbhag 12:30 PM – 3:30 PM.

  1. 1Assembly · 15–20 min

    Arrival

    Children come in and keep their bags on their chairs. Sanskrit shlokas play softly as they arrive, which makes the school feel calm, happy and welcoming.

  2. 2Assembly · 15–20 min

    Prayer and assembly

    Jana Gana Mana, the prayer of the month, the school pledge, affirmations, “all about today” and birthday celebrations.

  3. 3Assembly · 15–20 min

    Five-minute wake-up

    Taali yoga, jumping jacks, kavayat or a little dance. It freshens everyone up, and any sleepiness is gone!

  4. 4First session

    Shloka of the month

    Back in class, we chant the Sanskrit shloka of the month. It helps children speak clearly and improves their pronunciation.

  5. 5First session

    Attendance with a twist

    No “Yes teacher” here. Each day has a topic: if today is flowers, every child names a flower when their name is called. Revision happens during attendance itself!

  6. 6First session

    Orals

    As per the class: 1 to 100, ONE to HUNDRED, ABCD with phonics, and Hindi orals.

  7. 7First session

    Writing practice and parallel activities

    Each child practises writing as per their syllabus. Those who finish early pick a parallel activity, such as a puzzle kept in the corner. We have about 80 of them.

  8. 8Tiffin · ½ hour

    Tiffin and sharing

    We say a prayer before eating. Every day, each child shares some food with the friend on their right, and learns to share. Once the tiffin is finished, it is time for the slides and free play.

  9. 9Second session

    Project of the month

    Every month has a new project, such as community helpers. We talk about it, and on some days watch a short related video on the TV.

  10. 10Second session

    Rhymes, games and play

    Rhymes, indoor and outdoor games, the sandpit, puppet shows, and on some days a little more writing.

  11. 11Home time

    “Namaste teacher, bye teacher!”

    Before going home, every child says Namaste and goodbye to everyone.

Through the year

Celebrations and events

  • Birthdays celebrated at prayer time, with the whole school
